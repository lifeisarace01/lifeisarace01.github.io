Top 10 Shopping Bag Manufacturers in the USA in 2026

  • Product Introduction

Posted by Trimela On Sep 07 2026

Top 10 Shopping Bag Manufacturers in the USA in 2026 For a US retail shopping-bag program, start with the bag that must work at the checkout: the products it holds, how staff open and pack it, the appearance customers expect, and how stores replenish it. Only then decide whether the supplier needs paper-converting equipment, textile sewing, nonwoven conversion or film-bag production.

Our 2026 shortlist includes American Paper Bag, Novolex/Duro, ProAmpac, Ross & Wallace, Fischer Paper Products, Enviro-Tote, Unionwear, Bagmasters, Universal Plastic and Excellent Poly. Each has an official description of a relevant US manufacturing operation or US-made shopping-bag offering. Those descriptions do not establish domestic origin for every item in their catalogs.

This guide gives priority to retail paper carriers, grocery carryout and merchandise bags, then covers reusable and film alternatives. Buyers focused specifically on sewn promotional totes can also consult our USA tote-bag manufacturer comparison.

Editorial disclosure and review date: Trimela publishes this commercial buying guide. Company information was reviewed on September 7, 2026 using official manufacturer pages. The numbered list reflects product fit and the evidence available to a purchasing team; it is not an audited quality league table or the result of comparative purchase orders. Trimela is based in Ningbo, China and appears separately as an import sourcing option for American buyers.

What qualified a company for inclusion

We looked for three essentials: an official shopping or carryout bag offering, identifiable US manufacturing activity, and enough product information to form a useful sourcing question; a supplier offering only a US sales address without manufacturing evidence did not qualify.

There are no invented numerical scores. Ordering favors the paper and checkout applications central to this guide, followed by reusable and film specialists. Within those groups, fit depends on the actual brief.

1. American Paper Bag: custom retail paper carriers

American Paper Bag identifies manufacturing in Sugar Notch, Pennsylvania and Reno, Nevada. Its paper-bag offering includes customized shapes and dimensions, flat-paper, twisted-paper and die-cut handles, and water-based flexographic printing with up to eight colors. Interior printing and closure features also appear on its official paper-bag page.

When to shortlist it

Consider this route when the carrier is part of the retail identity and the structure needs to fit a specific product assortment. A bag for boxed cosmetics requires a different base and opening from a carrier for folded clothing.

What to settle in the RFQ

Request a structural sample that can hold the intended contents. Confirm the assigned plant, paper specification, handle configuration, print coverage, finish and packed-case dimensions. Have store staff test loading and carrying before approving a visually attractive design.

2. Novolex / Duro: paper formats for repeat replenishment

Duro is counted here within Novolex. The group’s 2026 paper-bag catalog covers handle shoppers, merchandise bags and grocery/SOS formats, with different printing routes. Its facility list identifies several US Duro operations as well as Rio Bravo, Mexico. Buyers requiring US manufacture should therefore identify the actual plant and product.

When to shortlist it

This is a relevant starting point for a chain that needs a family of paper bags across departments or store sizes. Standardization can simplify replenishment when the assortment is mapped to the right bag sizes.

What to settle in the RFQ

Separate stock sizes from custom-converted items and identify the printing process for each. Ask how substitutions are controlled, whether different plants may supply the same SKU, and how changes in paper shade, case count or print appearance are communicated.

3. ProAmpac: paper shopping bags within a packaging program

ProAmpac’s paper shopping-bag range identifies several US production facilities and offers natural-kraft or bleached-white constructions, multiple handles, printing up to eight colors and UV coating. Its stated applications include specialty retail, grocery, delivery and foodservice.

When to shortlist it

Consider ProAmpac when shopping bags sit inside a wider packaging procurement brief. The evaluation should still be specific to the carrier: expertise elsewhere in a group’s portfolio does not establish suitability for a particular bag.

What to settle in the RFQ

Request paper weight and grade, the selected handle, gusset dimensions, printing and coating details, and a trial case. If the proposed bag carries recycled-content or certification wording, ask for documentation that supports that exact construction and order.

4. Ross & Wallace: practical grocery and merchandise bags

Ross & Wallace Paper Products states that its products are manufactured at its Hammond, Louisiana facility. The range includes SOS grocery bags and sacks, heavy-duty bags and merchandise bags, with custom printing offered for grocery and merchandise formats.

When to shortlist it

The product emphasis fits functional carryout and routine replenishment. This can be useful where a reliable pack size and practical checkout operation matter more than elaborate finishes.

What to settle in the RFQ

Confirm whether the exact structure requires handles and whether they are offered. Ask how the bag opens from the packed stack, how its base supports the basket, and whether the chosen paper and printing suit the contents. A grocery sack and a premium rope-handle carrier should be evaluated as different products.

5. Fischer Paper Products: branded carryout through trade channels

Fischer describes US manufacturing and printing and traces its production history to Antioch, Illinois. Its product range includes handle bags and custom paper formats, with graphic support for branding. Its company information also explains a commercial route involving eligible resale businesses and distributors.

When to shortlist it

Food-led retailers, bakeries and cafes may find this range relevant when the shopping bag connects retail presentation with takeaway service.

What to settle in the RFQ

Establish account eligibility or the appropriate distributor early. Then define how the bag will be used: a carrier holding wrapped products is different from packaging that directly contacts food. Request the paper, handle, print, intended-use documentation and distribution arrangement needed for the application.

6. Enviro-Tote: reusable bags with a documented domestic sewing route

Enviro-Tote describes cutting, stitching and screen printing at its Londonderry, New Hampshire plant. Its manufacturing overview discusses custom sizes and several textile options. It says most fabric is domestically sourced, which leaves the origin of a particular fabric or component to be confirmed.

When to shortlist it

This route is relevant to museum shops, bookstores and retail programs selling or distributing a bag for continued use. The brief often involves hand feel, useful proportions and decoration that suits the fabric.

What to settle in the RFQ

Verify the selected material’s origin and composition, handle length, usable print area and care instructions. Test the finished bag with a realistic load. For a reuse proposition, agree on durability evaluation using our reusable shopping-bag testing guide.

7. Unionwear: sewn totes for union and merchandise programs

Unionwear describes manufacturing in Newark, New Jersey in its company story. Its bag range includes configurable sewn totes. A printed strap-and-pocket tote, for example, shows a gusseted base, printed handles, an adjustable strap and a zipper pocket.

When to shortlist it

Consider Unionwear when union procurement, a specific decoration treatment or a more developed merchandise bag is important to the brief.

What to settle in the RFQ

Confirm the union-label requirement, exact style, fabric and trim origins, decoration method and sales channel. A statement about US sewing does not by itself establish domestic origin for every component. Request a production sample in the intended material before committing to the print layout.

8. Bagmasters: selected domestic shoppers within a mixed catalog

Bagmasters offers a useful example of why origin must be checked by SKU. Its Grocery Shopper Tote Bag, item 1099-74, is described as made in the USA using imported fabric. The listed construction is a gusseted nonwoven-polypropylene shopper with reinforced handles. The broader catalog should be evaluated separately.

When to shortlist it

This route can suit buyers whose brief matches an existing shopper structure and who prefer catalog ordering over developing every structural detail from scratch.

What to settle in the RFQ

Identify which dimensions and components are fixed and which can change. Ask for the current minimum, decoration area, production schedule and origin statement. Confirm that an alternative color or substitute SKU carries the same substantiated origin description.

9. Universal Plastic: custom film shopping bags

Universal Plastic describes a California manufacturing facility and custom poly-bag production, including printed shopping and trade-show bags. Its company contact information identifies Ontario, California.

When to shortlist it

Consider a film specialist when the selected material is suitable and permitted for the destination and use. A merchandise carrier, event bag and regulated checkout bag may fall under different requirements.

What to settle in the RFQ

Specify resin construction, thickness, handle opening, reinforcement, gusset, seals, print and packing. Confirm the actual manufacturing location and evaluate the intended use against current state and local rules. A US manufacturing location does not establish permission to supply every retail checkout application.

10. Excellent Poly: larger printed handle-bag orders

Excellent Poly states that its products are manufactured entirely in the USA. Its die-cut handle bag page describes polyethylene/coextruded constructions, optional bottom gussets and custom printing. The company overview provides the manufacturing statement; buyers should request the current assigned facility address.

When to shortlist it

The product route is relevant to larger retail, event and promotional film-bag programs where specification and print setup can be planned in advance.

What to settle in the RFQ

Confirm the current quantity threshold and production window rather than treating website terms as permanent. Request a sample with the intended handle geometry, thickness and print coverage. Review local bag restrictions before allocating the style to a multi-state store rollout.

What “Made in USA” establishes—and what buyers must still check

The FTC explains that an unqualified US-origin claim requires US final assembly or final processing, all significant processing in the United States, and all or virtually all domestic components and ingredients. Qualified claims can describe a more limited US contribution when they are truthful and supported.

A US warehouse or local printing operation alone is insufficient evidence for an unqualified claim. See the FTC’s Made in USA guidance.

Ask the supplier for the wording it can substantiate on the quoted item. Keep three questions separate in the approval record: where the bag is made, where its important inputs originate, and what wording may appear on the product or marketing page.

Supplier statement What it tells the buyer Next verification step

“We are a US company” Corporate or commercial presence Identify the manufacturing site for the quoted item

“Printed in the USA” Location of a decoration step Establish where the undecorated bag was made

“Made in the USA of imported fabric” A qualified production description Match the statement to the exact SKU and evidence

“Made in USA” without qualification A broad origin claim Request substantiation supporting that wording

“Ships from a US warehouse” Stock or dispatch location Check origin, inventory ownership and substitution rules

These checks should happen before artwork is frozen.Changing an unsupported origin statement after printing can create an avoidable replacement or rework problem.

US checkout rules can change the material decision

California illustrates why retail location belongs in the RFQ. From January 1, 2026, covered stores generally may provide only compliant recycled-paper carryout bags at the point of sale, subject to the law’s definitions and exclusions; customers may bring reusable bags. This is not a universal prohibition on every plastic bag in every setting. The paper requirement moves to at least 50% postconsumer recycled material in 2028. Check the current CalRecycle bag requirements for the intended store and use.

For a multi-state program, have the buyer’s compliance team approve a destination matrix before purchasing. Record store category, bag function, required materials and markings, fee treatment where relevant, and the date of review. Reconfirm the applicable state and local requirements before rollout. This guide provides sourcing context rather than a nationwide legal determination.

Domestic production, US stock or direct import?

These are different purchasing choices, and some companies offer more than one. A domestic factory can have a queue; an imported bag can be immediately available from stock; a fully custom import can require advance development and transport planning. Ask for the actual schedule and availability behind the quote.

Run a store trial before approving a chain-wide order

Use a representative assortment of purchases and the actual packing space. Ask staff to open bags, load products, carry them and put them back into storage. Include the product combinations that expose weaknesses: a wide carton, a concentrated load, awkward corners or multiple packages with an unstable stack.

Record whether the opening stays accessible, the base remains stable, handles are comfortable and the print rubs against neighboring bags. Compare usable bags per shelf or drawer, rather than only bags per shipping carton.

Choose acceptance criteria before the trial.A paper bag may need a different handling and moisture test from a textile bag; reusable bags may need cleaning and repeated-carry evaluation.

A satisfactory store trial complements material and laboratory checks where required.

Keep environmental claims tied to the purchased SKU

Ask for the evidence behind the exact claim being considered: fiber or polymer composition, recycled percentage and its calculation basis, a current certificate where applicable, and the relationship between the documents and the shipment. A certification for an input or facility does not automatically cover the finished shopping bag.

Use our recycled-content verification guide when that attribute is part of the brief. For Trimela, certifications and environmental wording must be reviewed against the specific supply route and order; historical or upstream documents are not presented as current company-wide credentials.

Frequently asked questions

Which US manufacturer should I contact first for printed paper shopping bags?

Start with a paper-focused candidate whose products match your application. American Paper Bag, Novolex/Duro and ProAmpac offer relevant paper carrier routes; Ross & Wallace and Fischer add practical grocery, merchandise and carryout options. Ask two or three suitable candidates to quote the same brief.

Are all products from these ten companies made in the USA?

No. The list identifies documented US manufacturing offerings.Origin may differ by product and facility, and some catalogs combine domestic and imported items. Obtain confirmation for the quoted SKU, including component origins when they affect the intended claim.

Is Trimela one of the ten US manufacturers?

No. Trimela is a China-based supply partner and is discussed separately for American buyers evaluating imports. This distinction allows a retailer to compare sourcing choices without confusing supplier market coverage with manufacturing origin.

Can a bag printed in America be labeled Made in USA?

Printing location alone does not establish the bag’s origin. Review the bag’s manufacturing and component evidence and choose a supported statement using the FTC guidance linked above. Get qualified advice when the intended label requires interpretation.

Is a reusable bag always suitable for checkout distribution?

No. Suitability depends on the store, destination rules and product function as well as performance. A bag sold as merchandise or brought by a customer can be treated differently from one provided at checkout. Confirm the intended use before selecting material.

Is buying from a US company faster than importing?

It can be, but the useful comparison is the actual order schedule. Confirm stock availability, artwork approval, materials, production queue, shipping and receiving requirements. A supplier’s location alone does not establish the delivery date.

Can I buy directly from every manufacturer on the list?

Buying channels vary. Some accept project inquiries, some use distributors or resale accounts, and some offer catalog ordering.Confirm the commercial route before spending time on a detailed quotation request.

What minimum order should a retailer expect?

Minimums vary with conversion, material, dimensions, decoration and release structure. Request the minimum per design and color, then distinguish that from the quantity released to each store. A published minimum is a dated starting point for discussion.

What should be approved before the first production run?

Approve a written specification, physical sample where appropriate, artwork, origin and claim wording, inspection requirements, case pack and delivery responsibilities. Identify who can approve substitutions and how a material or plant change will be reported.

What should I send Trimela for a US project?

Send the bag’s role, destination states and store type, size, contents/load, material preference, artwork, quantity by design, packing instructions and required arrival date. Include origin restrictions and any claim or testing requirements at the start.
