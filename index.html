The studio

A small team that likes making things well

Trimela is a young workshop in Guangzhou. We design and produce custom bags, caps and everyday apparel for brands and distributors.

We prefer clear briefs, honest sampling, and production you can inspect before it ships.

  • Bags Totes, cosmetic bags, shopping bags and drawstring pouches — sampled before bulk.
  • Apparel T-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, jackets, pants, knits, denim, shorts, skirts and sunglasses.
  • Caps Baseball caps and bucket hats with the mark, color and packing you specify.
  • Sample to carton Color, stitch and hardware lock on the sample. Short runs and repeat orders ship from Guangzhou.

Sourcing

What a brand order actually needs

  • Flexible MOQ Short runs to open a style, then repeat orders when the collection lands.
  • Sample from a brief A sketch or a reference photo is enough to start. You approve a piece you can hold.
  • QC before packing Color, stitch and hardware are checked against the approved sample.
  • Guangzhou export Packed in Panyu with notes that travel with the carton — not a forwarded RFQ.

Compliance

Papers EU and US buyers put on the PO

A mill ticket for fabric does not cover hardware or print. We name the regulation, the article and the wearer, then attach mill copies and lab reports to that SKU — not a generic “we are certified” banner.

  • OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100
  • REACH EU chemicals
  • ISO 9001 Mill QMS
  • ISO tests 105 · 9227 · 17025

The sample matched bulk. Colour and stitch did not drift between the first carton and the repeat.

How we work

How a brand starts to look like itself

From a sketch or a reference photo to a wearable, shippable sample. Capacity is sized for short runs and repeat orders, not just one big drop.

  1. 01 Brief Quantity, size, artwork and a date — from a sketch or a reference photo.
  2. 02 Sample A piece you can hold, reject, or send to your customer.
  3. 03 Confirm Lock the approved sample. Bulk follows that piece, not the photo.
  4. 04 Inspect & ship Check finish, pack, and send from Panyu on an honest date.

Send a brief. Get a sample plan.

Tell us the quantity, material direction and destination. We reply with what we can make, what we cannot, and when a sample can leave Guangzhou.
