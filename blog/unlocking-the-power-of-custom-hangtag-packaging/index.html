Custom Hang Tags for Bags & Apparel: Specification, Printing & QC

  • Product Introduction

Posted by Trimela On Nov 27 2025

Buyer specification guide · Last reviewed 2 September 2026

Custom Hang Tags for Bags & Apparel: Specification, Printing & QCA hang tag is small, but the file behind it is not. For a retail-ready bag or garment, buyers must control the tag stock, size, print, finish, hole position, fastener, attachment point, copy, SKU data and packing method as one approved specification.

1. Hang tag, permanent label, insert or pack label?

Start by assigning each piece of information to the correct component. A hang tag is normally removable and visible at retail. A sewn-in or otherwise permanent label stays with the product. An insert sits inside a bag or package. A barcode sticker may be applied to the tag, polybag or carton. Combining all four into one undefined “label” is a common source of missed content and wrong attachments.

4. Artwork and dieline controls

The artwork file should show more than the visible front panel. Use an approved dieline with finished cut, bleed, safe zone, fold, hole centre, eyelet boundary, foil/varnish/emboss layers and any variable-data area clearly separated.

  • Supply vector logos and outlined or packaged fonts according to the agreed prepress workflow.
  • Keep small text, thin rules and QR/barcode modules away from cut, fold and hole zones.
  • Name spot colours consistently and include an approved physical colour reference when colour is critical.
  • Separate CMYK artwork, spot colour, white ink, foil, varnish, emboss and cut lines into unambiguous layers.
  • Record the file version, approval date and SKU assignment. Never replace a production file silently.
  • Check back-panel copy in every required language; do not assume the English layout will fit after translation.

The shopping-bag artwork and dieline guide provides the larger-format file logic. The Pantone, ink-drawdown and bulk colour guide covers physical colour approval, while the retail finishing guide goes deeper on foil, emboss and surface effects. This article applies those controls specifically to the hang-tag component.

5. Barcodes and QR codes: test the printed object

A barcode that looks correct in the PDF may fail after printing, lamination, die cutting or attachment. Request final data from the brand or retailer’s controlled system, generate the correct symbol, preserve the required quiet zone and verify the printed sample at its actual size. Dark bars on a light background are the safest starting point, but the applicable GS1 specification and trading-partner requirements control the final choice.

7. A 10-gate workflow from brief to bulk

  • Freeze the product/SKU matrix. List every colour, size, language and destination variant.
  • Issue the component brief. Record the 14 fields above and nominate the data owner.
  • Review feasibility. Confirm stock, print, finish, die, hole, fastener and attachment constraints.
  • Approve the dieline and digital proof. Check dimensions, layers, copy and version mapping.
  • Review a blank dummy where useful. Test scale, fold, hole, string and physical fit before decorated sampling.
  • Approve the printed tag. Compare colour, print, finish, cut, hole and variable data.
  • Approve an attached finished-product sample. Confirm orientation, removal, product marks and unit packing.
  • Freeze the approval record. Photograph the approved sample, sign/date the spec and archive the production files.
  • Inspect first-off and in-line output. Catch print, die-cut, attachment and SKU-mapping drift early.
  • Complete pack-out reconciliation. Match tag versions, product quantities, unit packs, carton labels and remaining components.

For the full product-development sequence, see the custom bag sample process and design-to-delivery manufacturing guide. They cover the entire item; the gates here prevent the hang tag from becoming an uncontrolled last-minute add-on.

9. What changes cost, MOQ and lead time?

No single MOQ, price or lead time applies to every hang-tag project. Trimela confirms commercial terms after the tag specification, quantities, artwork, packing and production schedule are reviewed. The main drivers are:

  • stock type, colour, thickness and whether it is standard or custom sourced;
  • finished size, custom shape, die and number of layouts imposed per sheet;
  • print sides, colour process, spot colours, white ink and variable data;
  • foil, emboss, spot coating, lamination, eyelets and other finishing steps;
  • number of SKUs, languages, barcode versions and quantities per version;
  • fastener material, length, colour and manual assembly complexity;
  • attachment labour and where it occurs in the packing sequence;
  • proof, dummy, printed sample, barcode verification or testing scope;
  • overage/shortage rule, individual packing and destination/carton sorting.

Quoting rule: send the tag quantity matrix and product quantity matrix together. A total of 10,000 products split across 40 small tag versions is not the same production problem as one 10,000-piece tag version. Final MOQ, price, sample cost and timing remain subject to written project confirmation.

12. RFQ checklist

Send these items with the product enquiry:

  • product type, SKU list, quantities by SKU and destination market;
  • hang-tag finished size, shape, stock, thickness and colour;
  • print sides, colours, finishes, hole/eyelet and fastener specification;
  • exact attachment point shown on the product drawing or sample photo;
  • vector artwork, dieline, final copy, languages and responsible approver;
  • barcode/QR data source, required symbol, scan/verification requirement;
  • unit packing, set configuration, carton sorting and destination labels;
  • sample route, approval deadline, target delivery date and Incoterm;
  • any material, performance, sustainability or compliance claim with its required evidence scope.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a hang tag and a product label?

A hang tag is usually removable and used for retail presentation or variable data. A permanent product label remains attached and may carry information required for the destination market. Define both separately in the BOM.

Can a barcode be printed directly on a hang tag?

Yes, when the symbol, size, quiet zone, colour, surface and data are appropriate. Test and, where required, verify the actual printed and attached tag—not only the PDF.

Which attachment is best for bags?

There is no universal best choice. The handle, zipper pull, seam loop, drawcord or another approved point may work depending on construction, presentation, removal and packing. Approve a finished sample.

Can Trimela provide custom hang tags with bags or apparel?

Trimela can review hangtags, labels, barcode packing and project-specific export packing within an OEM/ODM brief. Feasibility, material, finish, MOQ, price and timing are confirmed for the specific order.

What file format should we send?

Vector artwork is preferred for logos, cut lines, foil and other production layers. Also send the approved copy, fonts or outlined text, colour references and a controlled SKU/data file. Final prepress requirements are confirmed with the selected production route.

How should environmental claims on a hang tag be handled?

Treat every claim as evidence-dependent. Bind the wording to the specified material, percentage, certificate or test scope and responsible legal review. A brown stock, recycling icon or supplier statement alone does not prove a claim.

Should we order extra tags?

Agree an order-level allowance only after the SKU matrix, replacement need, packing process and inventory-control method are known. Uncontrolled excess tags can create version, claim and misuse risks.
