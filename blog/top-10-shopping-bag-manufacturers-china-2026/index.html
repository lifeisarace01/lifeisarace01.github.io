A seven-step China supplier verification workflow

The goal is not to collect the largest document folder. It is to create a traceable chain from the quoted entity to the goods that ship.

Step 1: reconcile the legal and commercial entities

Compare the business license, quotation, purchase order, bank beneficiary and exporter.If different entities appear, ask for a written relationship and responsibility map. A brand name or English trading name is not enough.

Step 2: map the actual production route

List material preparation, weaving or fabric supply, lamination/coating, printing, cutting, sewing/sealing, finishing, inspection and packing. Mark each step as in-house or external and record the site. This is more useful than asking only, “Are you a factory?”

Step 3: issue one comparable RFQ

Send identical technical and commercial fields to every candidate. If each supplier quotes a different fabric, handle, print or carton, the prices are not comparable. Use the shopping-bag tech-pack guide to close the gaps.

Step 4: approve in stages

Approve material and color first, then structure, then decoration, then the complete pre-production sample. Keep a signed or sealed reference with revision number and date. Photography is useful for communication but cannot replace a physical master where texture, color or construction is critical.

Step 5: bind claims to evidence

If the order uses “recycled,” “organic,” “certified,” “compostable,” “food contact” or another regulated or environmental claim, define the exact wording and required evidence before quotation. Check entity, site, material, percentage, scope, validity, transaction and destination-market requirements.

Step 6: define inspection and testing

Use measurable checkpoints for dimension, weight, print, color, seams, handles, closures, load, drop, odor, restricted substances and packing as applicable. Start with the bag QC checklist. Add the separate printing-control checklist when decoration risk is significant.

Step 7: verify export and carton readiness

Confirm HS-code responsibility, labels, packing list, marks, country-of-origin marking, fumigation or wood-packaging issues, carton dimensions, gross weight and Incoterm. The process in How to Import Custom Shopping Bags from China helps connect product approval with shipment execution.

Quality risks to test before mass production

Handle failure

Check attachment position, reinforcement, stitch/seal pattern and load method. Test the filled bag in a way that reflects lifting, carrying and repeated handling—not only a gentle static hang.

Print variation

Define color master, substrate, ink/process, viewing light, registration tolerance and rub/adhesion test. The approved appearance on white paper may not predict the result on a textured, colored or laminated bag.

Dimensional drift

State where and how dimensions are measured. Soft bags should be laid and conditioned consistently. Include handle drop and gusset, not only width and height.

Odor, migration or restricted substances

Requirements depend on material, ink, coating, use and destination market. Do not ask for a generic “all-compliance certificate.” Build a product- and market-specific test plan with qualified professionals or laboratories.

Premature wear

If reuse supports the marketing message, assess seams, handles, abrasion, washing or cleaning instructions and realistic user behavior. See Reusable Shopping Bag Durability and Reuse Testing for a test-plan framework.

Certification claims: verify scope, not logos

GRS, GOTS, FSC, BSCI, ISO and other names are not interchangeable. A management-system certificate, social audit, chain-of-custody certificate and product test answer different questions. They may apply to different entities, sites, products and dates.

LUCKYSTAR does not present any of these as a blanket company-wide certification for every shopping bag. When a buyer requires a particular claim, the team should confirm the current order route and available documentation before promise or publication. The same standard applies to every company in this list.

A defensible check asks:

  • Who is the certificate or report holder?

  • Which site and activity are covered?

  • Is the document current and independently verifiable?

  • Does the scope include the ordered product or material?

  • Is transaction or batch documentation required?

  • Can the buyer legally use the intended logo or wording?

Red flags when interviewing China shopping-bag suppliers

“Every process is in-house” without a process map

Ask the supplier to label the location of every important step.External processing can be acceptable, but undisclosed processing makes approval and corrective action difficult.

One certificate image offered for every SKU

Certificate scope is specific. If the name, address, site, material, activity, product or validity does not match, the document may not support the proposed claim.

A quotation that silently changes the RFQ

Watch for altered material weight, dimensions, handle length, print method, quantity break or packing. Require a deviation list rather than searching line by line.

A beautiful sample made by a different route

The pre-production sample should use the intended material and process. If a showroom sample comes from another customer, line or method, it is only inspiration.

Capacity promises without a schedule

Ask for line allocation, critical material lead time, sample approval date, production window, inspection date and shipment buffer. A monthly headline does not reserve capacity.

Environmental language without a defined boundary

“Eco-friendly,” “green” and “sustainable” are too broad on their own. Specify the attribute, percentage, evidence, lifecycle boundary, disposal condition and market. Avoid turning one material feature into a claim about the whole product.

Is LUCKYSTAR a factory or a trading company?

Which Chinese city is best for shopping bags?

It depends on the product. What MOQ should I expect?

There is no reliable category-wide MOQ. It changes with material, color, size, print, tooling, process, packing and current line conditions. Treat website figures as snapshots and request an order-specific written quotation.

How do I know whether a supplier owns its factory?

Check the business license, site address, assessment or audit reports, video call, production records and an independent on-site audit if risk justifies it. More importantly, map each process and identify who controls external steps. Ownership alone does not guarantee quality.

Can one supplier make paper, canvas and woven-PP bags equally well?

Possibly through a multi-process organization or managed supply network, but the production routes are materially different.Evaluate each route separately and ask for the assigned facility, sample, equipment/process, quality plan and relevant evidence for every SKU.

How should I verify GRS, GOTS or FSC claims?

Verify the certificate holder, site, standard, scope, product/material coverage and validity through the applicable official system where available. Ask whether transaction documentation is required. Do not infer that a company logo or general certificate covers the order.

What should a golden sample control?

It should identify revision, dimensions, material, color, print, handles, seams or seals, closures, accessories, finish and packing as applicable. Pair the physical sample with a signed specification because some characteristics cannot be preserved reliably in one sample.

When should I use a third-party inspection?

Use it when order value, schedule, market risk or supplier history makes independent verification worthwhile. Define the sampling plan, defect classes, measurements, functional tests, packing checks and reporting rules before production, not after goods are finished.

How can I reduce reorder variation?

Keep controlled artwork, color references, material specifications, approved samples, process parameters, supplier/site allocation and inspection records. Require written approval for substitution. Review aged physical masters before relying on them for color.

Are transparent shopping bags waterproof?

Do not assume so. A film may resist water, while seams, openings, zippers or needle holes permit ingress.Define the intended exposure and test method, and use precise wording such as water-resistant only when supported.
