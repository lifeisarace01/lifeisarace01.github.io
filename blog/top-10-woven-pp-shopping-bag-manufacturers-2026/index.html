What “woven PP shopping bag” should mean in an RFQ

Polypropylene resin is melted and extruded into tapes. Those tapes are oriented and woven into a grid-like fabric. A retail bag may use the fabric unlaminated, extrusion-coated, or laminated to a reverse-printed BOPP film. Panels are then cut and sewn, handles attached, edges bound and accessories added. The finished construction—not the base polymer name alone—determines the bag’s behavior.

Component Decisions to specify Why it affects the finished bag Evidence to retain

Base woven fabric Polymer, color, tape specification, weave density and base-fabric GSM Controls hand feel, coverage, tear propagation and dimensional stability Material specification, roll identification and approved swatch

Print film or coating Film resin, thickness, matte/gloss finish and print side Affects color, abrasion, moisture response and end-of-life composition Layer declaration, artwork proof and approved printed reference

Lamination Adhesive or extrusion route, bond level and treatment Weak bonds can bubble, tunnel, peel or delaminate at folds Process parameters, peel criterion and defect limit

Bottom support None, sewn base, PP board or other insert Changes stability and may add another material to disclose Component list and assembly sample

Build a woven-PP RFQ that produces comparable quotations

Define the use case and geometry

State the contents, target net load, frequency of use, carrying distance, retail environment and desired service life. Give finished width, height and gusset; handle length and drop; top and bottom construction; seam allowances; insert requirements; folding method and dimensional tolerance. A photograph is not a dimensioned specification. Use How to Prepare a Custom Shopping Bag Tech Pack to create one controlled revision.

Separate base-fabric GSM from finished laminate weight

Ask for base woven-fabric GSM, film thickness or GSM, coating/adhesive contribution and total finished material weight. “120 gsm” may refer to different measurement points. Also define permitted variance and how incoming or in-process checks will be recorded.Material weight affects strength, cost, carton mass and environmental assessment; see How Material Weight Affects Shopping Bag Environmental Performance.

Freeze the print and lamination route

For photographic artwork, reverse printing on BOPP film followed by lamination is common. Define film finish, number of colors, artwork coverage, color standard, registration tolerance, barcode or fine-text requirement, scuff expectation and approval sequence. For simpler graphics, ask whether direct flexographic or screen printing is technically and commercially more suitable. Compare routes in Printing Methods for Woven PP Shopping Bags and our broader Custom Shopping Bag Printing Methods guide.

Put the load path into the specification

The handle, attachment stitches, top hem, side seam, gusset and bottom seam must carry the load together. Specify handle material and width, attachment depth, stitch pattern, reinforcement, thread and edge binding.A high-GSM body can still fail early at a short handle overlap or poorly secured seam.

Define inspection and acceptance before production

Agree on sampling level, defect classes and measurable criteria for dimensions, print, contamination, odor, lamination, stitching, handle location, packing and tests. Treat “reusable,” “recyclable” and recycled-content claims separately

Woven PP can be engineered for repeated service, but “reusable” should be supported by a defined use scenario and finished-bag durability evidence. “Recyclable” depends on the complete construction and an accessible collection/reprocessing route; a laminated, bound, printed bag may be harder to process than a single-material resin label suggests. Recycled content is a different claim again and requires traceability for the claimed percentage and components.

Do not turn a supplier’s website badge into your brand’s claim.Check the legal holder, site, material category, scope, validity and any order-level transaction document. Frequently asked questions

Is woven PP the same as non-woven PP?

No. Woven PP is made from interlaced polypropylene tapes, while common spunbond non-woven PP forms a sheet from bonded filaments.They differ in appearance, load distribution, converting and print options.Neither is automatically more sustainable without comparing mass, service life, reuse and end of life.

Is BOPP lamination necessary?

No. It is useful for high-coverage, photo-like reverse printing and for modifying surface behavior. It also adds a layer and a lamination process. Simple artwork, tactile preference or end-of-life goals may support an unlaminated construction. Decide from the project requirement.

Which GSM should a woven PP shopping bag use?

There is no universal optimum. Size, load, weave, film, seams, handles, inserts and target life all matter. Ask suppliers to quote one defined construction and validate it with a finished-bag test rather than selecting GSM in isolation.

Can woven PP shopping bags be printed in photographic quality?

Reverse gravure printing on BOPP film followed by lamination is commonly proposed for broad, detailed artwork. Actual color, registration, fine detail, scuff behavior and finish should be approved through production-representative proofs and samples.

Are woven PP bags waterproof?

Do not use that absolute claim without a product-level method. A laminated panel can resist liquid penetration, while stitch holes, open tops, seams or closures may still allow water entry. Specify the exposure and test criterion you need.

Are woven PP shopping bags recyclable?

Polypropylene may be technically recyclable, but a finished bag can include film, inks, adhesive, thread, webbing, binding and inserts. Local collection and processing determine practical recyclability.Describe the complete construction and avoid unqualified disposal claims.

How should a buyer verify recycled PP content?

Request the claimed percentage and whether it applies to base cloth, film, handles or the whole bag. Verify the supplier chain, valid certificate scope where relevant, input/output records and order transaction evidence. Do not infer recycled content from color or appearance.

What should be approved before mass production?

Approve the controlled specification, artwork, layer structure, color reference, construction sample, tolerance table, test method, defect standard, packing and change-control rule. Record which approval governs if references conflict.
