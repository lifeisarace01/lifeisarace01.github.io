Top 10 Shopping Bag Manufacturers in Europe in 2026

  • Product Introduction

Posted by Tendlyn Studio On Sep 07 2026

Top 10 Shopping Bag Manufacturers in Europe in 2026 Which European shopping bag manufacturer belongs on your shortlist? The answer depends on whether you need a machine-made paper carrier for hundreds of stores, a hand-finished boutique bag, or a reusable shopper for a retail campaign. A familiar company name is less useful than a production route that fits the bag and the countries where it will be used.

Our 2026 selection covers Papier-Mettler, Fiorini International, Smith Anderson, Dempson, Bagfactory, Extrapack, RIEDLE, Ecosac, Ecobag Network and Ace Packaging. Their official websites describe relevant European manufacturing activities and shopping-bag products. The comparison below explains what differentiates them and what to establish before requesting a production quotation.

About this selection: This is a commercial editorial guide published by LUCKYSTAR, with manufacturer information reviewed on September 7, 2026. Numbers identify entries, not independently measured quality positions. We have not conducted comparative factory audits or purchase trials. LUCKYSTAR is a China-based custom supply partner, discussed separately for European buyers considering imports.

This shortlist uses three filters: a relevant shopping or carrier bag range, a first-party description of European manufacture, and enough product detail to identify a useful purchasing application. We count related brands and group operations as one entry. Inclusion does not establish where every product in a company’s catalogue is made.

1. Papier-Mettler: broad retail carrier capability

Papier-Mettler describes an owner-run packaging manufacturing business based in Morbach, Germany. Its company overview explains that its own production is complemented by trading partners. Its paper packaging manufacturing page describes artwork, printing-plate preparation, ink mixing, printing and converting.

The paper and plastic carrier range makes it worth investigating when a retailer needs several bag formats under one procurement brief. That breadth should lead to a product-by-product discussion rather than an assumption that the entire catalogue is German-made.

For a useful quotation, identify which site will convert the bag, which steps are outsourced, and whether the chosen handle and dimensions belong to an established format. Ask how artwork references are maintained if several production routes support the programme.

2. Fiorini International: paper shopping bags for premium presentation

Fiorini International distinguishes manufacturing companies in Italy and the Czech Republic from its trading companies in other markets. Its range includes automated paper shopping-bag production and handmade luxury paper carriers.

This is a relevant candidate for fashion, cosmetics and premium retail briefs where the bag’s presentation is part of the purchase experience. Machine-made and hand-finished routes can serve different design and quantity requirements, so specify the intended construction before comparing prices.

Ask the team to identify the manufacturing company for the proposed bag and explain the finishing sequence. Review the paper, print, surface treatment, top fold and handle attachment together. A reference image of a luxury bag is not a complete manufacturing specification, particularly when coatings or decorative processes change the feel and appearance.

3. Smith Anderson: everyday paper carryout formats

Smith Anderson identifies a purpose-built manufacturing facility in Kirkcaldy, Scotland. Its product selection includes flat and satchel bags, SOS bags and paper tape-handled carriers; the company website describes its Scottish manufacturing base.

The range is relevant to daily retail and food-to-go purchasing, where opening, loading and case replenishment are important alongside print appearance. Start with the normal basket of goods rather than choosing a bag solely from its nominal dimensions.

Distinguish a carrier for already packaged products from packaging intended to touch food directly. Define the actual use, load, handling conditions and printing requirements, then request supporting specifications. A paper-bag manufacturer’s food-sector experience does not automatically establish suitability for every food-contact application.

4. Dempson: UK paper carriers and retail packaging

Dempson describes itself as a UK paper-bag and carrier manufacturer. Its Dempson Bag for Retail is presented as a paper carrier for high-street retail, alongside its wider paper packaging and printing activities.

Consider this manufacturer for a UK retail programme that needs a practical branded paper carrier. Its website discusses several operating functions, so buyers should distinguish bag manufacture from printing, sheeting, warehousing and distribution. Two distribution locations should not be treated as evidence of two bag-making factories.

Specify the product load, handle arrangement and case pack, and ask which operation will make the quoted bag. Review a printed sample for handling as well as colour: an attractive print result does not answer whether the bag packs efficiently or carries the intended contents comfortably.

5. Bagfactory: paper and reusable nonwoven routes

Bagfactory states that it established its own manufacturing plant in Lithuania in 2015. It describes machine-made paper carriers and reusable nonwoven bag production, including PP and PET constructions.

This combination makes it a useful candidate when the purchasing team is deciding between a routine paper carrier and a reusable synthetic shopping bag. The two routes should be evaluated against different service requirements, not treated as equivalent simply because both can display a logo.

For the nonwoven option, establish the polymer, material weight, construction, handle reinforcement and intended use. For paper, establish the grade, dimensions, handle and finish. Any recycled-content percentage needs its own evidence and calculation basis. The manufacturer’s contact location should also be distinguished from the precise plant assigned to the order.

6. Extrapack AD: several material families from Bulgaria

Extrapack AD identifies factories in and near Veliko Tarnovo, Bulgaria, separately from its Sofia trade office and warehouse. Its company presentation and technology overview describe paper-bag lines, printing and finishing, as well as textile and nonwoven conversion.

The range includes paper, polyethylene and textile promotional carriers. It is worth considering when one campaign requires different formats—for example, a retail paper bag and a promotional textile shopper—with a shared design language.

Ask which production division will handle each item and whether the same artwork needs adapting to different print processes. Confirm film, lamination, textile and handle specifications separately. A coordinated visual identity is useful, but it should not conceal different material evidence, performance tests or end-of-life considerations.

7. RIEDLE: premium paper bags with detailed finishing

RIEDLE states that its bags are produced in Germany and the EU. Its paper carrier bag manufacturing information covers custom paper carriers, printing and premium construction options.

RIEDLE is a relevant shortlist entry for boutiques, gifting and premium branded packaging where handles, top finishing and the overall hand feel deserve detailed approval. Its geographical wording matters: “Germany and the EU” is not a promise that every quotation will use German production.

Request the proposed country of manufacture and a sample showing the actual paper and finishing combination. Discuss how bags are protected inside cartons, particularly where decoration or dark surfaces can mark through contact. Include those packing details in approval instead of leaving them until the bags are finished.

8. Ecosac / AP Holding: machine-made paper carriers in Poland

Ecosac describes Polish paper-bag manufacture, automated production and custom print support. Its website names AP Holding S.A. as the entity and identifies Katowice as an office and production location; the corporate seat is a separate piece of company information.

The offering is relevant to buyers looking for standardised branded paper carriers. A clear specification can help compare an established size against a fully custom format without overlooking differences in paper, handles or case quantities.

Confirm the legal seller, production address and exact dimensions on the quotation. If brown kraft appearance is part of the design, still ask for the paper’s actual composition: colour alone does not substantiate recycled content. Review the print on the selected paper rather than approving artwork only against a white-screen background.

9. Ecobag Network: varied print routes for paper-bag programmes

Ecobag Network describes in-house digital, screen, flexographic and offset printing alongside paper-bag production. An April 2026 company announcement identifies its production facility in Przyprostynia, Poland. The announcement is used here as location evidence, not as independent verification of a current certificate.

The range spans flat and twisted handles, unhandled bags and premium formats. Buyers comparing several designs or print-run sizes can use that range to discuss which production method fits each SKU.

Ask whether the proposed printing occurs before or after bag conversion, how that affects the printable area, and which dimensions are standard. Match the approved proof to the actual paper, handle and finish. Membership of a larger group should not lead to counting related operations as additional shortlist entries.

10. Ace Packaging: Irish paper-bag manufacture

Ace Packaging describes paper-bag manufacturing and flexographic printing within Ace Group. The group’s packaging page states manufacture in Ireland; the business is based in Blessington, County Wicklow.

Its twisted-handle, SOS and V-shaped paper formats make it relevant to Irish retail and food-service projects. Begin by choosing the appropriate structure, since a flat food-service bag and a handled shopping carrier solve different problems.

Request the available paper and size options, print proof and intended-use documentation. Confirm the actual production slot and delivered quotation. Proximity can be useful operationally, but it should not be translated into an automatic claim of faster delivery, lower cost or a smaller environmental footprint.

What European buyers should check in 2026

For packaging placed on the EU market, the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2025/40, entered into force on February 11, 2025 and generally applies from August 12, 2026. Requirements are phased in: do not assume every future obligation started on the general application date. The European Commission provides the current overview and implementation resources.

Ask the responsible compliance reviewer to identify the provisions relevant to the bag, use, destination and launch date. Keep that determination with the product specification. The UK and other non-EU European destinations require their own assessment; a European manufacturing address does not settle the rules for every market.

This guide identifies sourcing questions rather than providing a legal determination for a specific order.

Frequently asked questions

Which manufacturers should a premium retailer approach first?

Fiorini International and RIEDLE are relevant starting points for premium paper presentation. Provide the desired construction, finishes and quantities so each can identify a suitable production route. Compare decorated physical samples before selecting a finish combination.

Does a European supplier guarantee European-made shopping bags?

No. Manufacturing, importing, distribution and local printing can coexist within one business. Request the proposed producing entity and site for the exact bag, and require approval before an origin-changing substitution.

Are the UK companies in this guide EU manufacturers?

They are European manufacturers in the geographical sense, but the UK is not an EU member. Keep manufacturing location and destination-market requirements separate when writing purchase orders and reviewing documentation.

Should I choose paper or a reusable textile shopper?

Start with the function. Routine carryout, premium presentation and a bag intended for repeated personal use are different briefs. Compare useful capacity, construction, customer behaviour and evidence for the proposed claims rather than selecting one material as universally preferable.

Is European production always the lower-impact option?

Geography alone cannot establish that conclusion. A defensible comparison needs consistent assumptions about the finished bag, production, transport, use and end of life. Avoid converting a shorter route or a material label into an unsupported overall environmental claim.

Can one artwork master serve stores in several countries?

Often the brand design can be shared, but required wording, languages, labels and destination instructions should be reviewed separately. Maintain approved market versions and confirm which files are used for each order release.

Does LUCKYSTAR offer a European manufacturing route?

This article makes no such claim. LUCKYSTAR is presented as a China-based custom supply alternative for buyers whose purchasing policy allows imports. The actual production arrangements must be confirmed with the quotation.

What information should I send for a LUCKYSTAR quotation?

Send the intended use, destination countries, dimensions, preferred material, handles, artwork, quantities by design, packing requirements and arrival dates. Identify any required origin, material claims or tests before sampling so the proposal can address them explicitly.
