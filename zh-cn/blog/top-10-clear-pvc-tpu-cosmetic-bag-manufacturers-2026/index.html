It does not assume that PVC is automatically cheap and poor, that TPU is automatically sustainable, or that every clear pouch is leakproof or accepted at an airport checkpoint.

PVC or TPU? The grade matters more than the label

PVC and TPU are families of formulations, not two fixed recipes. A soft PVC film may use a particular plasticizer package, stabilizer and surface treatment. A TPU film varies by chemistry, hardness, thickness and additives. Clarity, odor, tack, yellowing, cold performance, printing and welding can change from one grade to another.

Do not approve broad claims such as “non-toxic,” “eco-friendly,” “biodegradable,” “anti-yellowing” or “TSA approved” without a defined basis. The US Transportation Security Administration describes a one-quart, clear, plastic, zip-top bag under its 3-1-1 liquids rule; it does not issue a universal factory certificate for custom cosmetic pouches. The final traveler and checkpoint decision still matter.

“0.3 mm clear TPU” is not a complete specification. Then define what the shopper may see: haze, gels, fisheyes, black specks, scratches, flow marks, crease whitening and distortion.

Control RF welding as a process window

A transparent welded seam must be strong enough without becoming burned, narrow, wavy or optically distracting. The result depends on film grade and thickness, electrode geometry, energy, pressure, dwell/cooling, alignment, contamination and machine condition. Approve a process window—not a single attractive sample made by an experienced technician.

A good seam coupon, a narrow under-weld and an overheated wavy weld make the visual limit concrete for production teams.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between clear PVC and clear TPU cosmetic bags?

PVC is often selected for cost-efficient, established transparent constructions. TPU is often considered for softer hand, flexibility and a premium feel. Actual clarity, odor, scratch behavior, cold performance, yellowing and weldability depend on the grade and thickness. Compare finished prototypes made to the same dimensions.

Is TPU always more sustainable than PVC?

No. TPU is not automatically bio-based, biodegradable, recyclable in a local collection system or lower impact over the product life. Ask for full composition, durability evidence, the standard behind any environmental claim and realistic end-of-life context. Avoid substituting one polymer name for a life-cycle claim.

Can a transparent cosmetic bag be described as TSA approved?

TSA publishes a 3-1-1 liquids rule describing a one-quart, clear, plastic, zip-top bag. Buyers should avoid implying that TSA certified a custom product unless they have a specific, valid authorization. Design to the practical requirement and phrase travel claims carefully.

What film thickness should a clear cosmetic pouch use?

There is no universal gauge. The decision depends on pouch size, shape, required stiffness, folding, zipper load, weld width, trim and desired hand. Approve nominal gauge plus tolerance and compare a finished filled sample; a thicker film can still fail if the weld or corner geometry is poor.

Are RF-welded clear cosmetic bags leakproof?

A qualified weld can create a continuous film joint, but the complete bag may still leak at the zipper, end stops, sewn trim or attachments. Define whether the requirement concerns external splash, internal spill containment or another exposure, then test the closed finished bag under an agreed protocol.

How should a factory inspect clear bags?

Use normal and backlit viewing with defined distance, lighting and defect zones. Combine that appearance standard with gauge checks, weld-width and strength monitoring, zipper operation, dimension review, print adhesion, filled handling and packed-storage samples. Keep physical limit samples for subjective defects.

Which restricted substances matter for PVC and TPU bags?

The answer depends on destination, intended user, components, contact and customer requirements. Plasticizers, colorants, inks, adhesives and hardware finishes may all need assessment. Have a qualified laboratory or compliance professional create the applicable list, then map reports to the actual BOM and production variants.

What MOQ should buyers expect?

Public references in this shortlist range from 500 pieces for selected styles to 1,000 pieces or more. A custom film color, print, zipper tape, metal puller or new welding tool can create a higher component minimum than the bag assembly. Request price and feasibility at two or three quantities.
