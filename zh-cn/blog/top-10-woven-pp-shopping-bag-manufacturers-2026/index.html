Top 10 Woven PP Shopping Bag Manufacturers in 2026 Woven polypropylene shopping bags are bought as finished carriers, but their quality begins with a multilayer system: extruded PP tapes, woven fabric, optional BOPP film, inks and lamination, cut panels, edge binding, handles, thread and sometimes a bottom insert. Two quotations can both say “120 gsm laminated woven PP bag” while describing products with very different strength, appearance and reuse potential.

For 2026 sourcing, ten companies worth investigating are Zhejiang Hongyang Packaging, Wenzhou Mou’an Packaging, Fei Fei, CPC Vietnam, Sapphire Packaging, WEPAC, K-Packings, Cady Bag Company, PEMA Verpackung and Baya Polypack. This is a practical procurement shortlist, not a claim that one supplier is universally better than another.

What “woven PP shopping bag” should mean in an RFQ

Polypropylene resin is melted and extruded into tapes. Those tapes are oriented and woven into a grid-like fabric. A retail bag may use the fabric unlaminated, extrusion-coated, or laminated to a reverse-printed BOPP film. Panels are then cut and sewn, handles attached, edges bound and accessories added. The finished construction—not the base polymer name alone—determines the bag’s behavior.

1. Zhejiang Hongyang Packaging Co., Ltd.

Zhejiang Hongyang Packaging presents a Wenzhou-based operation covering PP woven fabric, standard woven bags, BOPP-laminated woven bags, paper-plastic composite constructions and woven shopping bags. Its website shows retail-style reusable carriers alongside industrial packaging, which makes it relevant when a buyer wants to examine both upstream woven-material control and downstream conversion.

The useful sourcing question is not simply whether the company “makes woven bags.” Ask which facility extrudes tape, weaves fabric, prints film, laminates, cuts and sews the exact shopping-bag SKU. Request a process map, dated equipment list and a sample-traceability explanation. If the proposal mentions recycled PP or a certification, verify the current certificate, holder, site, product scope and transaction documents instead of relying on a product-page label.

Best-fit investigation: high-volume BOPP-laminated retail carriers or projects that benefit from reviewing the fabric-to-bag route. Verify before nomination: legal seller, assigned plant, film and ink structure, seam specification, actual capacity window and finished-bag test plan.

2. Wenzhou Mou’an Packaging

Mou’an Packaging describes PP woven fabric rolls, woven bags, BOPP-laminated bags and reusable shopping bags from Wenzhou. Its retail product page discusses reverse-printed BOPP film laminated to woven PP and offers custom dimensions and printing, making the company a logical technical comparison for graphic-heavy grocery or promotional carriers.

Treat online values for material weight, mesh, load, MOQ, lead time and print colors as starting points rather than commitments. A useful RFQ should identify whether GSM refers to base cloth or the finished laminate, whether film thickness is included, the print cylinder and plate assumptions, edge construction, handle anchoring and tolerances. For repeat business, ask how the supplier stores color references, cylinder data and construction revisions.

Best-fit investigation: custom laminated shopping bags with broad image coverage. Verify before nomination: current manufacturing site, which operations are internal, lamination-bond acceptance, color tolerance, cylinder ownership and terms for repeat orders.

3. Fei Fei

Fei Fei’s woven-PP product page presents both laminated and unlaminated polypropylene woven shopping bags within a wider custom-bag portfolio. That breadth can be useful to a brand comparing woven PP with non-woven, polyester, cooler-bag or other carrier routes through one commercial contact.

Portfolio breadth does not prove that every process is controlled at one facility. Buyers should ask where fabric is woven, where printed film is produced, who laminates it and where the final sewing occurs. A factory video is helpful, but an order-linked flow chart, approved sample, inspection plan and production records are stronger.Confirm the bag’s intended use rather than accepting broad claims such as “waterproof” or “recyclable” without a defined construction and test basis.

Best-fit investigation: brands that want to compare multiple bag materials before locking the specification. Verify before nomination: process ownership, material traceability, sample-to-bulk controls and order-specific compliance evidence.

4. CPC Vietnam

CPC Vietnam publicly describes woven PP or PE, non-woven PP, RPET and other reusable bag routes, with weaving, lamination, printing and assembly among the processes referenced on its site. Its multi-site narrative may suit large retail programs or sourcing strategies that seek Vietnamese production.

Multiple sites also increase the importance of allocation control. The purchase contract, audit, material approval and inspection must refer to the same named legal entity and assigned factory. Do not infer that every capability, audit or certificate shown online applies to every CPC location. Ask who controls resin or fabric procurement, whether film printing and lamination occur at the assigned site, and how subcontracting or overflow would be approved.

Best-fit investigation: scaled woven-PP shopping-bag programs requiring coordinated material and conversion stages. Verify before nomination: factory allocation, certificate site scope, production capacity for the requested month, process subcontracting, social-compliance documentation and final inspection responsibility.

5.Sapphire Packaging

Sapphire Packaging describes PP woven, PP non-woven, RPET, cooler and other reusable bag categories manufactured through its Vietnamese operation. It may be relevant to buyers who want one development conversation across different reusable-carrier structures.

The category list should lead to a construction comparison, not a sustainability conclusion. For woven PP, request the base-cloth specification, print-film and lamination structure, gusset format, piping, thread and handle reinforcement. If recycled content or a certification appears in a proposal, check the certificate holder, valid dates, material category, site and order-level transaction evidence. A certificate logo on a website is not enough to support a claim on your bag.

Best-fit investigation: multi-material retail programs or buyers comparing woven PP against RPET and non-woven options. Verify before nomination: exact woven-fabric source, internal versus external processes, certificate applicability, load-test method and defect limits.

6. WEPAC Joint Stock Company

WEPAC’s PPW01 product page identifies PP woven material, a stated weight range, gravure printing, matte or glossy BOPP film and handle options. Its public material is clearly oriented toward reusable retail shopping bags rather than only industrial sacks.

The published ranges are useful for scoping but too broad to purchase against.Convert them into one controlled SKU: finished dimensions, base-fabric weight, film thickness, total weight, handle construction, seam allowance, artwork coverage and test load. If a product page states a load range, request the test fixture, dwell time, number of cycles, conditioning and failure definition before repeating that number in marketing.

Best-fit investigation: BOPP-laminated grocery and retail bag programs with detailed graphic decoration. Verify before nomination: selected SKU construction, lot traceability, stated load evidence, color-control method and actual production slot.

7. K-Packings

K-Packings presents Vietnam-based woven PP shopping bags with various fabric weights, weave densities, top and bottom finishes, printing and customization choices. The detailed parameter menu can help an importer recognize how many variables sit behind one product name.

Some stated specifications span light retail carriers and much heavier packaging. Require the supplier to identify a specific reference construction that matches your use, then verify the legal contracting entity and manufacturing workshop. Claims about ISO, SGS, UN or environmental performance must be checked for document type, certificate holder, product scope, site and validity; a broad table entry is not an order-level approval.

Best-fit investigation: projects needing broad woven-PP construction choices from Vietnam.Verify before nomination: entity and site, shopping-bag conversion experience, quoted specification boundaries, certificate files and realistic tolerance/test criteria.

Cady Bag Company states that it manufactures woven polypropylene fabrics and bags in the United States and describes BOPP consumer-packaging capability. Best-fit investigation: North American programs seeking woven-PP and BOPP packaging expertise. Verify before nomination: actual retail-tote route, country of origin, sourced components, decoration options, MOQ and production timeline.

9. PEMA Verpackung GmbH

Build a woven-PP RFQ that produces comparable quotations

Define the use case and geometry

State the contents, target net load, frequency of use, carrying distance, retail environment and desired service life. Give finished width, height and gusset; handle length and drop; top and bottom construction; seam allowances; insert requirements; folding method and dimensional tolerance. A photograph is not a dimensioned specification. Use How to Prepare a Custom Shopping Bag Tech Pack to create one controlled revision.

Separate base-fabric GSM from finished laminate weight

Ask for base woven-fabric GSM, film thickness or GSM, coating/adhesive contribution and total finished material weight. “120 gsm” may refer to different measurement points. Also define permitted variance and how incoming or in-process checks will be recorded.Material weight affects strength, cost, carton mass and environmental assessment; see How Material Weight Affects Shopping Bag Environmental Performance.

Freeze the print and lamination route

For photographic artwork, reverse printing on BOPP film followed by lamination is common. Define film finish, number of colors, artwork coverage, color standard, registration tolerance, barcode or fine-text requirement, scuff expectation and approval sequence. For simpler graphics, ask whether direct flexographic or screen printing is technically and commercially more suitable. Compare routes in Printing Methods for Woven PP Shopping Bags and our broader Custom Shopping Bag Printing Methods guide.

Put the load path into the specification

The handle, attachment stitches, top hem, side seam, gusset and bottom seam must carry the load together. Specify handle material and width, attachment depth, stitch pattern, reinforcement, thread and edge binding.A high-GSM body can still fail early at a short handle overlap or poorly secured seam.

Define inspection and acceptance before production

Agree on sampling level, defect classes and measurable criteria for dimensions, print, contamination, odor, lamination, stitching, handle location, packing and tests. Our Shopping Bag Printing Quality Control Checklist and printing-defect prevention guide help convert subjective comments into checkpoints.

Treat “reusable,” “recyclable” and recycled-content claims separately

Woven PP can be engineered for repeated service, but “reusable” should be supported by a defined use scenario and finished-bag durability evidence. “Recyclable” depends on the complete construction and an accessible collection/reprocessing route; a laminated, bound, printed bag may be harder to process than a single-material resin label suggests. Recycled content is a different claim again and requires traceability for the claimed percentage and components.

Do not turn a supplier’s website badge into your brand’s claim.Check the legal holder, site, material category, scope, validity and any order-level transaction document. Use qualified language that names the product and evidence. Frequently asked questions

Is woven PP the same as non-woven PP?

No. Woven PP is made from interlaced polypropylene tapes, while common spunbond non-woven PP forms a sheet from bonded filaments.They differ in appearance, load distribution, converting and print options.Neither is automatically more sustainable without comparing mass, service life, reuse and end of life.

Is BOPP lamination necessary?

No. It is useful for high-coverage, photo-like reverse printing and for modifying surface behavior. It also adds a layer and a lamination process. Simple artwork, tactile preference or end-of-life goals may support an unlaminated construction. Decide from the project requirement.

Which GSM should a woven PP shopping bag use?

There is no universal optimum. Size, load, weave, film, seams, handles, inserts and target life all matter. Ask suppliers to quote one defined construction and validate it with a finished-bag test rather than selecting GSM in isolation.

Can woven PP shopping bags be printed in photographic quality?

Reverse gravure printing on BOPP film followed by lamination is commonly proposed for broad, detailed artwork. Actual color, registration, fine detail, scuff behavior and finish should be approved through production-representative proofs and samples.

Are woven PP bags waterproof?

Do not use that absolute claim without a product-level method. A laminated panel can resist liquid penetration, while stitch holes, open tops, seams or closures may still allow water entry. Specify the exposure and test criterion you need.

Are woven PP shopping bags recyclable?

Polypropylene may be technically recyclable, but a finished bag can include film, inks, adhesive, thread, webbing, binding and inserts. Local collection and processing determine practical recyclability.Describe the complete construction and avoid unqualified disposal claims.

How should a buyer verify recycled PP content?

Request the claimed percentage and whether it applies to base cloth, film, handles or the whole bag. Verify the supplier chain, valid certificate scope where relevant, input/output records and order transaction evidence. Do not infer recycled content from color or appearance.

What should be approved before mass production?

Approve the controlled specification, artwork, layer structure, color reference, construction sample, tolerance table, test method, defect standard, packing and change-control rule. Record which approval governs if references conflict.
