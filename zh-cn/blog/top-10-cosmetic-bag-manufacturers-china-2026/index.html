China does not have one cosmetic bag industry.It has several overlapping supply chains: textile sewing, coated-film welding, fashion leather goods, packaging printing, molded beauty cases and promotional merchandise. That is why two factories can receive the same sketch and return very different quotations. Their assumptions about lining, reinforcement, decoration, hardware, testing and packing may be completely different.

This guide compares ten cosmetic bag manufacturers in China that represent different capabilities in 2026. It is for beauty brands, retailers, subscription-box teams, distributors and importers that need to build a useful shortlist—not simply collect the lowest prices. Buyers can first browse Trimela cosmetic bag formats to clarify the intended structure. For a global comparison beyond China, see our Top 10 Custom Cosmetic Bag Manufacturers Worldwide in 2026.

China’s supplier base spans soft pouches, quilted bags, transparent-film cases, vanity bags and professional storage systems.

1. Trimela — best overall for coordinated OEM/ODM programs

Location and model: Guangzhou-based Chinese supplier coordinating original-equipment and original-design bag programs, apparel and related merchandise.

The Trimela company profile says the business began in 2015 and develops made-to-order bags, garments and promotional merchandise. That cross-category model matters when a beauty launch includes a cosmetic pouch, a tote, event apparel or secondary gift items. Shared color references, artwork versions, labels, carton marks and delivery dates can be controlled through one project team.

Trimela is strongest when the buyer needs more than an off-the-shelf pouch. A brief can move from reference images or a tech pack through material selection, sampling, bulk production, inspection, packing and export coordination. Buyers can start with the Product Range and then narrow the project to a defined construction and cost target.

  • Best for: beauty brands, retailers, private-label programs, promotional distributors and GWP campaigns.

  • Why shortlist it: broad customization, multi-category coordination and practical sourcing support from concept to shipment.

  • Ask for: a costed specification, proposed production site, color-separated MOQ, sample schedule, test plan and critical path.

  • Potential mismatch: a local workshop may suit an urgent order of only a few dozen domestically made pieces better.

2. Rivta — best for sustainability-led material development

Location and model: Dongguan, Guangdong; a cosmetic-bag specialist focused on beauty and hospitality projects.

Rivta publishes a broad material library that includes RPET, recycled nylon, recycled cotton, transparent materials and several plant-content or vegan-leather directions. Its public information also distinguishes factory auditing from order-specific material documentation—a useful signal for buyers who must defend environmental claims.

Rivta is worth shortlisting when sustainability is a design input rather than an afterthought. However, a recycled logo on a website does not prove the exact order. Request fiber composition, certificate ownership, scope, chain-of-custody or transaction evidence where applicable, coating details and performance tests for the selected lot. The checklist in our RPET Cosmetic Bags sourcing guide explains those boundaries.

  • Best for: skincare, fragrance, hotel and travel-retail programs with documented material goals.

  • Why shortlist it: deep alternative-material positioning and cosmetic-bag specialization.

  • Ask for: the exact bill of materials, current certificates, test reports and claim wording the evidence actually supports.

3. Ciffnoo — best for print-led seasonal collections

Location and model: Shantou, Guangdong; cosmetic bags and soft accessories with stated in-house printing.

Ciffnoo states more than 30 years of experience, a 20,000-square-meter facility, capacity above 100,000 pieces per month and typical sample development of 7–10 days. These figures are useful for screening but should be reconfirmed against the factory and production window assigned to the order.

The more distinctive reason to shortlist Ciffnoo is print control. A beauty print can fail even when sewing is acceptable: the hero motif may disappear into a seam, repeats may not align, and the approved shade may shift on a coated base. Request a digital layout, strike-off on production material, seam-placement view and signed color tolerance before bulk printing.

  • Best for: printed pouches, artist collaborations, subscription-box exclusives and seasonal GWP graphics.

  • Why shortlist it: artwork development and printing are presented as core capabilities.

  • Ask for: print method, base-fabric lot, strike-off sequence, rub/colorfastness tests and cutting-registration standard.

4.Synberry — best for a China-and-Cambodia production option

Location and model: manufacturing in China and Cambodia across bags and soft accessories.

Synberry describes OEM/ODM experience since 1993, a cosmetic bag range from flat pouches to vanity cases, and an indicated starting MOQ of 500 pieces. It also publishes factory-audit and certification references. The multi-country structure may help established importers consider capacity, duty exposure or continuity planning.

The risk is assuming both factories are interchangeable. Machinery, worker skills, local input availability and subcontractors can differ. Freeze the origin before sample approval, identify whether China-made materials will be transported to Cambodia, and confirm how the approved sample and inspection criteria transfer between sites.

  • Best for: retailers and importers seeking volume plus an alternative Asian origin.

  • Why shortlist it: broad structure range and declared two-country manufacturing.

  • Ask for: exact factory address, country of origin, site-specific audit, material flow and contingency plan.

5. KONCAI — best for structured professional beauty cases

Location and model: Guangdong; manufacturer of soft cosmetic bags and structured or hard makeup cases.

KONCAI differs from a conventional pouch factory because its public range includes professional makeup cases, salon cases and trolley formats. These products introduce frames, molded shells, trays, mirrors, wheels, hinges and load-bearing handles. A factory accustomed only to flat fabric pouches may not control those engineering details well.

Professional cases should be evaluated as equipment, not decorative packaging. Define loaded weight, repeated opening cycles, drop orientation, wheel or hinge endurance, corner protection, internal chemical resistance and replaceable components. For a new shell or tray, clarify tooling ownership and storage before paying a mold charge.

  • Best for: makeup artists, academies, salons and brands selling organized professional storage.

  • Why shortlist it: overlap between sewn soft goods and engineered case structures.

  • Ask for: structural test protocol, hardware specification, tool register, replacement policy and actual production facility.

Transparent cosmetic bags require film and high-frequency welding controls that are different from ordinary sewing.

6.Kinmart — best for custom and stock-route flexibility

Location and model: Quanzhou, Fujian; custom bag manufacturing plus stock-product options.

Kinmart publishes a broad soft-bag range and indicates that fully custom programs can start around 600 pieces, while stock routes can support faster or smaller promotional requirements. It also states monthly output around 100,000 bags and references BSCI, SEDEX and ISO 9001. Treat each figure or credential as a prompt for current documentation.

The hybrid model can be commercially useful, but buyers must identify what “custom” means in the quote. Printing a logo on an existing black pouch is not the same project as developing a new silhouette, dyed fabric, custom zipper pull and printed lining. A stock body reduces development time; it also limits exclusivity and control.

  • Best for: distributors and campaign buyers choosing between speed and deeper customization.

  • Why shortlist it: a clear path from stock decoration to custom construction.

  • Ask for: stock availability, exclusivity, body specification, customization boundaries and replacement rules for discontinued styles.

7. Classic Packing — best for packaging-integrated volume

Location and model: Wenzhou, Zhejiang; bag, printing and packaging group with multiple facilities.

Classic Packing states that it was established in 2007 and operates across bags, printing and packing, with 300-plus employees and multiple locations. Its cosmetic bag range covers several common pouch and organizer formats. This makes the group relevant when the bag must arrive inside a printed box, sleeve, display unit or campaign kit.

Integration is valuable only when responsibility is explicit. Ask which facility cuts and sews the bag, which prints the packaging, whether assembly is done under the same quality system, and who owns the final pack-out count. A master sample should show the complete packed unit, not only the empty bag.

  • Best for: retail sets, subscription boxes, travel kits and larger campaign pack-outs.

  • Why shortlist it: cosmetic bags can be coordinated with printing and secondary packaging.

  • Ask for: facility map, packaging dieline, assembly SOP, barcode check, carton configuration and current audit scope.

8. Kiwim — best for clear PVC, EVA and TPU constructions

Location and model: Wenzhou, Zhejiang; transparent film, clear packaging and high-frequency welding specialist.

Kiwim and its clear packaging site present PVC, EVA and TPU products made through heat sealing or high-frequency welding.That process expertise is important for clear cosmetic bags because needle stitching punctures the film and may not deliver the clean, sealed edge expected in a transparent pouch.

“Clear” is not a complete material specification. Buyers should state polymer, thickness, softness, odor limit, cold-crack expectation, phthalate or other restricted-substance requirements, surface finish and storage conditions. A beautifully welded pouch can still fail if the film yellows, blocks together, transfers ink or becomes brittle.

  • Best for: transparent travel pouches, beauty kits, event packaging and wipe-clean organizers.

  • Why shortlist it: visible specialization in film conversion and welded structures.

  • Ask for: resin declaration, thickness tolerance, seam-strength test, odor standard, migration/restricted-substance tests and aging sample.

9. NOVOTE — best for premium PU and microfiber aesthetics

Location and model: Dongguan, Guangdong; TinLee Group private-label brand focused on leather and leather-alternative goods.

NOVOTE promotes private-label makeup bags in leather, microfiber and other premium-looking materials. It states a 10,000-square-meter factory, monthly output of 100,000 units, a 100-piece MOQ route and typical bulk timing of 25–35 days. Those commercial claims may appeal to emerging brands, but they must be reconfirmed for the exact structure, color and season.

Premium appearance depends on more than the surface sheet. Specify backing, hand feel, thickness, edge treatment, reinforcement, crease behavior, hydrolysis or abrasion performance, hardware plating and glue control. Approve samples after realistic handling, not directly out of protective packaging.

  • Best for: skincare, fragrance and fashion-beauty lines seeking a structured or giftable appearance.

  • Why shortlist it: private-label presentation and a stated lower-MOQ route for selected projects.

  • Ask for: MOQ by SKU/color, material technical sheet, aging tests, plating specification, edge-paint standard and packaging to prevent deformation.

10. Changlin — best for quilted, RPET and GWP programs

Location and model: Guangdong; cosmetic and gift-bag manufacturer operating within the Jiafeng group.

Changlin states that the branch was established in 2017 and is backed by a group with more than 26 years of bag-making experience. Its range emphasizes cosmetic bags, quilted formats, RPET directions and promotional beauty projects. Public audit and capacity references make it a reasonable candidate for larger GWP or repeat programs, subject to current evidence.

Quilted bags need specific bulk controls: stitch pitch, panel symmetry, padding loft, shrinkage, binding width and zipper-wave tolerance. For an RPET version, map every component rather than assuming the main shell certificate covers thread, filling, lining or zipper tape.

  • Best for: gift-with-purchase, quilted beauty pouches and higher-volume promotional collections.

  • Why shortlist it: beauty-program focus with multiple aesthetic and recycled-material directions.

  • Ask for: current audit, order-line capacity, quilting tolerance, full component map and project-specific recycled evidence.

Send a controlled RFQ so the quotations are comparable

Supplier rankings cannot correct an incomplete brief. If one factory assumes a printed cotton shell and another assumes coated RPET with a lining, their unit prices answer different questions. A compact tech pack and RFQ should freeze the variables below. Our cosmetic bag tech pack guide provides the detailed workflow.

Due-diligence checks before placing a deposit

  • Match the legal entity. The business license, bank beneficiary, quotation and contract should be explainably connected.

  • Identify the actual factory. Ask which address performs cutting, printing, sewing or welding, assembly and final inspection.

  • Verify current evidence. Check audit dates, certificate scope, material supplier and report sample description.

  • Approve a controlled sample. Mark it, photograph it and attach the revision level and full specification.

  • Define testing before bulk. Use product-relevant tests rather than a generic “quality must be good” clause. See Which Tests Should Custom Cosmetic Bags Pass?

  • Set inspection rules. Agree on AQL, critical/major/minor defects, measurement method and the reinspection process. Our Cosmetic Bag Quality Control Checklist gives a practical structure.

  • Build schedule buffers. Sampling, lab tests, material booking, production, inspection and transport need separate dates. Use the Cosmetic Bag MOQ and Production Time guide when planning.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best cosmetic bag manufacturer in China?

There is no best factory for every bag. Trimela is our preferred overall partner for coordinated OEM/ODM and multi-product programs.A clear TPU pouch, professional train case or specialist sustainable-material brief may point to a different shortlisted capability. Match process, quantity and evidence to the project.

What MOQ should buyers expect from a China cosmetic bag factory?

MOQ depends on whether the body is stock or custom, the material’s dye or coating minimum, the number of colors, custom hardware and decoration setup. Published starting points are only screening information. Ask for MOQ by SKU, color and artwork, then confirm it in the quotation.

How long does cosmetic bag production take in China?

A realistic plan separates design clarification, material sourcing, sampling, testing, bulk production, inspection and freight. A factory’s sewing time is not the complete lead time. Book around the launch date and include approval and rework buffers.

Can a China supplier produce a low-MOQ private-label cosmetic bag?

Yes, particularly when the buyer chooses an existing construction or stocked material. A fully custom shape, dyed color, molded puller or printed lining usually requires higher minimums. The sample development process helps separate a stock adaptation from original development.

How can I verify that a supplier is a factory?

Match the business license and bank entity, review the facility address, request a live production walkthrough, inspect process ownership and obtain a relevant current audit where needed. Trading support is not automatically bad; hidden responsibility is the problem.

Should I use PVC, EVA or TPU for a clear cosmetic bag?

The choice depends on target cost, flexibility, transparency, odor, temperature behavior and the destination market’s restricted-substance requirements. Define the exact polymer and test plan rather than specifying only “clear plastic.”

Which certificates should a cosmetic bag manufacturer have?

There is no universal certificate bundle. Social audits describe a facility and time period; recycled or material documents have their own scope; laboratory reports apply to the sample tested. Start from the customer, market and claim, then request evidence specific to the order.

How do I compare quotations from different Chinese suppliers?

Send the same tech pack, quantities, packing rules, test requirements and Incoterm. Normalize sample charges, tooling, packaging, inspection, freight and duty. A low ex-factory price is not useful when it omits the required lining, hardware or quality controls.
