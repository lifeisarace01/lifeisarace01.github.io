定制gift bag guide: format, brief, samples & qc

定制gift bag guide: format, brief, samples & qc。广州 Trimela 按项目打样与大货。

Last reviewed: September 2, 2026

A custom gift bag is not one fixed product. It may be a paper carrier handed over at checkout, a small drawstring pouch around jewelry, a textile tote that becomes part of the gift, a zippered beauty pouch inside a campaign set, or one component in a coordinated box-and-bag presentation. Calling all of these products “gift bags” can produce quotations that are impossible to compare.

This guide gives sourcing, merchandising and packaging teams a cross-format decision process: choose the format around the packed gift, write one controlled brief, approve the filled presentation and define bulk acceptance. It does not duplicate the wider supplier framework in the Custom Bag Manufacturer Buyer’s Guide or the brand-system work in Custom Bags as a Branding System.

A real product photograph from Trimela’s current independent-site range. A tote can become part of a gift program, but its material, dimensions, artwork and construction still require project-specific approval.

Quick answer: what should you send a custom gift bag manufacturer?

  • The packed product: dimensions, weight, fragility, surface sensitivity and the number of items in each set.
  • The handover moment: retail checkout, e-commerce unboxing, corporate gifting, gift with purchase, event distribution or direct mail.
  • The selected format: paper carrier, drawstring pouch, textile tote, zippered pouch or a coordinated combination.
  • A controlled specification: finished dimensions, material construction, colour, handles or closure, reinforcement, logo method, labels, inserts and individual packing.
  • A filled sample requirement: the bag should be approved with the intended gift, tissue, box, insert and closure—not only while empty.
  • Written acceptance criteria: appearance, measurements, function, branding, pack-out, carton and evidence requirements.

Price, MOQ, sample timing and production timing should be confirmed after these details are reviewed. They are not universal promises across every gift-bag construction.

2. Measure the gift before sizing the bag

Bag dimensions copied from another project can fail as soon as tissue, ribbon or a rigid box is added. Record the largest packed state, not only the product itself. A paper carrier also needs insertion clearance and a base that supports the box. A drawstring pouch loses usable height to the cord channel and gathered closure. A zippered pouch needs enough opening length to insert the widest item. A textile tote may need gussets or reinforcement if the kit is bulky.

3. Build an RFQ that makes quotations comparable

A useful RFQ separates confirmed requirements from options that still need supplier input. Reference photographs are helpful, but label what you want from each image: “use the flat cotton cord,” “match this upright proportion,” or “do not copy the printed artwork.” Do not send a mood board and assume every visual detail has been approved.

4. Choose decoration after the surface and format are fixed

A logo technique cannot be selected independently from the bag. Paper, open-weave fabric, coated textile, plush material and synthetic leather accept different processes and show fine artwork differently. Seams, folds, gathers, handles and zipper paths reduce the usable decoration area. Approve the logo at its finished physical size on the actual surface.

  • For the full method comparison, use Custom Shopping Bag Printing Methods.
  • For artwork files, bleed, folds and production zones, use How to Prepare Shopping Bag Artwork and Dielines.
  • For placement and usable print area, use Shopping Bag Logo Placement and Print Size Guide.
  • For paper-carrier foil, embossing and spot UV, use Foil Stamping, Embossing and Spot UV for Luxury Shopping Bags.
  • For the wider presentation decision, use the Luxury Retail Shopping Bag Finishing Guide.

Colour names such as “warm gold,” “ivory” or “forest green” are not production standards. Record the colour reference, substrate and approval route. A screen proof can approve layout, but it cannot prove sheen, relief, ink coverage, foil adhesion or the way a fabric texture breaks a fine line.

5. Approve a filled sample and a golden pack-out

An empty bag can hide the most expensive problems. A carrier may bow once a rigid box is inserted. A pouch may close cleanly while empty but leave too little cord when filled. A logo may look centered on a flat panel and shift visually after the gusset expands. Tissue may cover the artwork or make the handles too short to gather comfortably.

  • Material or colour approval: confirm the proposed surface, colour and construction before judging decoration.
  • Artwork proof: freeze revision, size, position and colour separation.
  • Prototype: check the structure and usable volume with actual contents or dimensionally accurate dummies.
  • Decorated sample: inspect the logo on the intended material and completed bag.
  • Golden pack-out: assemble the gift exactly as fulfillment will—contents, tissue, insert, label, closure, individual protection and carton orientation.
  • Pre-production record: capture the approved sample, bill of materials, measurements, artwork revision, packing method and permitted changes.

The Custom Shopping Bag Sample Development Process explains the carrier route in detail. For sample scope, charges, revision control and handoff, use Custom Sample Bag Manufacturer: Scope, Cost & Handoff. A sample photograph is useful for communication, but when touch, colour, fit or relief matters, retain a physical approval master.

Run a short packing-station trial before freezing the sample

A presentation approved in a meeting room may still be awkward in fulfillment. Ask the people who will assemble the gift to pack several sets in the intended sequence. Record which component is picked first, how the bag is opened, where tissue or an insert is placed, whether barcode labels remain visible, how long cords or ribbons take to arrange, and how the completed unit is protected before cartoning.

The trial should answer operational questions that a beauty shot cannot. Can the product be inserted without scraping the print or lining? Does the zipper opening accept the rigid inner box? Does tissue catch in the drawstring channel? Can the handles lie flat without crushing the bow? Is the correct artwork easy to identify when several SKUs share one bag shape? Does the assembled bag remain stable while waiting for the next packing step?

Update the golden pack-out with the result. Use numbered photographs, component names, quantities, orientation and carton layers. If a change to the product, inner box, insert or label affects fit, reopen the relevant approval instead of treating the bag as an isolated component. This small pilot is especially useful when a gift program combines items from different vendors or when final assembly happens in another country.

6. Evaluate supplier evidence, not adjectives

A “renowned,” “premium” or “professional” supplier label is not evidence. Ask how the supplier will control the specific construction and pack-out you are buying. For a coordinated gift program, responsibility across components matters as much as the individual bag.

8. What changes cost, MOQ and lead time?

Commercial terms depend on the confirmed format and order. Important drivers include material construction, custom colour, bag size, reinforcement, handles or closure, number of artwork positions, printing or finishing route, tooling, quantity split by SKU, sample rounds, component assembly, individual packing, inspection, destination and delivery basis.

A coordinated set can introduce minimums at more than one level: the bag material, printed component, ribbon or cord colour, hardware, insert, box and carton may each have different production constraints. Ask the quotation to state which assumption drives the offered quantity and what changes when colours or artworks are split.

For total custom-bag economics, use How Much Do Custom Bags Cost?. For carrier-specific cost drivers, use How Much Do Custom Shopping Bags Cost?. The workflow from specification through delivery is covered by Custom Bag Manufacturing Process: From Design to Delivery.

RFQ checklist for a custom gift bag project

  • gift contents, packed dimensions, total weight and surface sensitivity;
  • channel, recipient, handover moment and intended presentation;
  • preferred format and any acceptable alternative;
  • external and usable internal dimensions, gusset and opening;
  • material construction, colour, lining and reinforcement;
  • handle, cord, ribbon, zipper or other closure details;
  • vector artwork, finished logo size, position, colours and method;
  • tissue, insert, hangtag, barcode, individual packing and carton requirements;
  • required sample stages, approval records and physical master;
  • bulk inspection scope, order-specific test or document requirements;
  • quantity by SKU, destination, Incoterm and target delivery date.

All commercial terms remain subject to the final specification, material availability, artwork, packing, production schedule and written confirmation.

Frequently asked questions

Is a paper bag or fabric bag better for gifts?

Neither is automatically better. Choose around the contents, handover moment, protection, presentation, intended use, logistics and evidence requirements. Approve the complete packed result.

How much extra space should a gift bag have?

There is no universal allowance. It depends on the product geometry, insertion direction, tissue or filler, closure, usable internal space and desired presentation. Test with the actual packed item or a dimensionally accurate dummy.

Should we approve an empty bag sample?

An empty sample is useful for construction review but is not enough. Approve a filled sample and a golden pack-out so fit, closure, handle behavior, logo position and presentation are visible together.

Can one supplier coordinate the bag, pouch, box and insert?

It may be possible for a project, but confirm which components are within scope, which operations or partners are used, who controls cross-component colour and dimensions, and who owns final pack-out inspection. Do not infer an owned production line from a broad product range.

Can a product photo prove that a gift bag is sustainable or compliant?

No. A photograph can show visible construction and appearance. Material composition, recycled content, certification, safety, performance and market compliance require order-specific evidence tied to the relevant product, material, entity and validity period.

What should the manufacturer quote include?

The quotation should identify the product specification, quantity split, material and decoration assumptions, sample or tooling charges, packing, inspection basis, Incoterm, timing assumptions, validity and exclusions. Clarify what triggers a revised price or schedule.
